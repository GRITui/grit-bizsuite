Cross-app margin & labor-efficiency aggregator — pulls POS revenue, inventory FIFO COGS, and taskboard completion data over HTTP (never a direct database query) and requires the custom_reporting addon.

This dashboard reads from grit-pos, grit-inventory and grit-taskboard through the cross-app aggregator, which is gated behind the custom_reporting addon (available from the GROWTH tier up). Ask your org owner to add it, or sign in with an account whose Grit Passport session includes it.
