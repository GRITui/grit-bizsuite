Grit BizSuite Dashboard

Cross-app data visualization dashboard — pulls POS revenue, inventory FIFO COGS, and taskboard completion data over HTTP (never a direct database query), and renders it as KPI tiles and charts. Requires the custom_reporting addon.

Session & range

🔒 Custom reporting addon required

This dashboard reads from grit-pos, grit-inventory and grit-taskboard through the cross-app aggregator, which is gated behind the custom_reporting addon (available from the GROWTH tier up). Ask your org owner to add it, or sign in with an account whose Grit Passport session includes it.

Daily revenue — per-day COGS/margin aren't available from the inventory COGS endpoint (period totals only), so the bars show revenue

Revenue composition — period totals: gross margin vs. inventory COGS as a share of POS revenue

Export

Downloads the aggregate rows (summary + daily revenue) as a CSV for further analysis in a spreadsheet tool of your choice.
